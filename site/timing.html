10⁻⁷ – 10⁻⁵ stransport

One hop: a message changing hands

The sender writes the payload once, in place, in shared memory. Only a descriptor moves: into the receiver's ring, then a bump of its bell. A sleeping receiver needs the kernel to wake it. A spinning one is already watching the bell, so the hop costs what it takes for a few cache lines to cross between cores.

10⁻⁶ – 10⁻³ scontrol

One control cycle: from a state to its command

Every millisecond the joints publish their state and a controller answers with a command. Until milestone 19 the command waited for the next tick, a full period. Now the joints wait for the answer within the tick, up to a 300 µs deadline, and apply it at once. The rest is how the two sides wait.

A simulated pendulum (keel-sim) and a PID (keel-pid) at 1 kHz; times are from sampled keel trace chains. Decisions 0027 and 0029.

Loops of one period tick on multiples of it on the machine's clock, whenever they started: the two arms' bus masters are in phase without being told. phase_us: 900 puts the drive's report 100 µs before their tick.

The numbers live in site/measurements.js; their sources are the benchmarks in docs/architecture.md and decisions 0027 to 0029.
